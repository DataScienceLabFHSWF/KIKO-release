AI-powered knowledge & competence platform

The knowledge nuclear decommissioning needs — preserved, taught, passed on.

KIKO is an AI-powered learning and knowledge platform for the decommissioning of nuclear facilities. It preserves expert knowledge, makes it accessible through natural language, and teaches it — adapted to each person's level.

What it's about

Decommissioning spans decades. The knowledge it requires must not be lost.

Decommissioning nuclear facilities demands trained staff and permanently available expertise — at a time of skills shortage. KIKO gathers decommissioning knowledge, keeps it current, and passes it on through modern language models to everyone who needs it.

Knowledge preservation

Experts' know-how is preserved in AI models and stays available long-term — even after knowledge-holders leave the field.

Early-career support

Students, doctoral researchers and career changers get up to speed faster — guided by AI assistants that act like mentors.

Internationalisation

Knowledge can be translated into other languages and used in multilateral projects — a basis for international cooperation.

First version — available now

Try the platform

KIKO's first version is a learning platform with courses in two views: one for instructors and one for learners. You can ask questions about the learning material, common misconceptions are surfaced, and practice and test questions are answered with AI feedback.

  • Two views — an instructor view and a learner view of the same course.
  • Courses with learning material — structured content you can ask questions about directly.
  • Common misconceptions — frequent misunderstandings are named and resolved.
  • Practice & test questions — with individual AI feedback on your answer.

What KIKO delivers

A learning system that adapts to the person

Information isn't simply presented — it's aligned to each person's needs, for knowledge gains with maximum learning effect.

Level-adapted learning

The AI guides users along their level of knowledge and offers help across all relevant topics.

Individual AI feedback

Topic-specific feedback closes knowledge gaps precisely — building an optimal learning curve.

Knowledge vault

Existing sources are enriched with the knowledge of key experts — a living, growing knowledge base.

Active learning agents

Autonomous AI agents carry the expertise of individuals and step in as digital lecturers during learning.

Source fidelity

Every output can be questioned: the original passages and the sources used are shown for verification.
